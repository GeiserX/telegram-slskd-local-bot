# Related projects

Music pipeline:

- [slskd-transform](https://github.com/GeiserX/slskd-transform): bulk upgrade of lossy files to lossless FLAC through Soulseek.
- [audio-transcode-watcher](https://github.com/GeiserX/audio-transcode-watcher): transcodes a lossless library into other audio formats as files arrive.
- [quality-gate-encoder](https://github.com/GeiserX/quality-gate-encoder) (formerly jellyfin-encoder): 720p HEVC, H.264 or AV1 copies of videos for Jellyfin.

Other Telegram bots:

- [paperless-telegram-bot](https://github.com/GeiserX/paperless-telegram-bot): manage Paperless-ngx documents through Telegram.
- [jellyfin-telegram-channel-sync](https://github.com/GeiserX/jellyfin-telegram-channel-sync): keeps Jellyfin access in step with Telegram channel membership.
- [Telegram-Archive](https://github.com/GeiserX/Telegram-Archive): Telegram backup with a local web viewer.
- [AskePub](https://github.com/GeiserX/AskePub) (archived): a Telegram bot for ePub annotation with GPT-4.
- [telegram-delay-channel-cloner](https://github.com/GeiserX/telegram-delay-channel-cloner) (archived): relays messages between channels with a delay.
